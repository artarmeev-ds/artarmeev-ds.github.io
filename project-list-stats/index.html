What people do on the list

Share of each Portal's list users who took the action — normalised so the two compare despite different windows.

The two lists look different, but people do the same things — most gaps were how events are named or split, not behaviour. Download is the biggest action on both (~62–65% of list users); it didn't show as one bar because it's split across per-asset events (tour_assets_downloaded_new for the sketch/assets, plus separate estimate and report downloads) — the chart uses the main one. Share and Edit happen on both too (tour_shared_public, edit_button_clicked). The one real difference is Search — new-only instrumentation, so it can't appear on the old Portal.

Same shape on both Portals: Visibility dominates (~52–57%) and the top three cover ~80%. The one shift on the new Portal is Period jumping to #2 (~15% vs 6%) — Phoenix users filter by recency more. So don't hide filters wholesale — keep Visibility visible (it's a primary lens, not an occasional filter), surface Period and Loss type as quick options, and fold the tail (Requested service, Property type, Office) behind a Filter control with active-filter chips.

What it means for the redesign

  1. The list is the daily hub — make it fast.
    Tens of thousands of users a month; load, scan and search speed is the priority.
  2. Find → open is the core loop.
    Opening a project must be effortless from the row.
  3. Downloading finished assets is the #1 action.
    ~62–65% of list users download a sketch/assets — keep a one-click download on the row.
  4. Keep the other row actions visible too.
    Sharing, ordering a sketch, opening the tour editor, estimates & reports are all common — actions aren't secondary.
  5. Filtering is a minority path.
    ~10–12% of list users filter; prioritise fast scan and search over heavy filter UI.
