Layout · density & radius

Tighter rhythm, softer edges

Compresses the spacing scale by ~15% (28→24, 24→20, 20→16) so more sections fit above the fold, then bumps card radii from 8px to 16px on desktop and 20px on mobile to compensate visually. Shadows go softer and farther. Net effect: more information per viewport, friendlier surfaces.

Typography · larger hierarchy

Bolder type, clearer hierarchy

Steps the heading scale up one notch (h1 24→28, h2 20→22, h3 16→18) and pushes body from 14→15. Medium weight rises from 500 to 600 so titles read decisively at-a-glance — useful for triage workflows where adjusters scan many projects per session.
